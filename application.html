General Rules:

  • Application is open to all Secondary 1 to Secondary 6 students across Hong Kong.
  • Participants are required to shoot a video (in MP4, MOV, WMV, AVI format etc.) under 2 minutes to showcase their artistic talent. Selected participants will receive a confirmation email on 23/6.
  • The submission period is from 22 May 2026 to 19 June 2026. Any late submission will not be accepted.
  • Participants may apply either as individuals or as a group. Group submissions are limited to a maximum of 5 members. Each group submission should only be submitted once.
  • Participants should upload their video submission onto Google Drive and submit the respective link through the Google Form below. Please change the settings of "General access" to "Anyone with the link".
  • All individual, group, band and ensemble performances must be a full-body shot.
  • All participants must be in proper attire. Revealing garments, attire featuring offensive graphics or language should be avoided.
  • The use of music, lyrics or spoken word containing references to controlled substances, depictions of violence or suggestive sexual themes is strictly prohibited.

規則：

  • 本活動開放予全港中一至中六學生參加。
  • 參加者須拍攝一段片長兩分鐘或以下的視頻（格式應為MP4、MOV、WMV、AVI等）以展示藝術才能。獲選者將於2026年6月23日獲電郵通知。
  • 提交期限為2026年5月22日至2026年6月19日。逾期遞交將不獲受理。
  • 參加者可以個人或小組形式參與活動。小組形式每組上限為5人。每個小組只需遞交視頻一次。
  • 參加者須將視頻上載至Google Drive，並透過以下的Google表格提交相關連結。請將「一般存取權」的設定更改為「任何知道連結的使用者」。
  • 所有個人、小組、樂隊及合奏表演必須採用全身拍攝。
  • 所有參加者須穿著得體，不得穿著暴露或帶有冒犯性圖像、語言之服裝。
  • 嚴禁使用提及受管制藥物、描繪暴力，或帶有暗示性色情主題的音樂、歌詞或口語。
