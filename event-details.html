Mission 任務

To discover and nurture diverse artistic talents young performers by providing a shining stage for them to showcase their unique style and creativity. Through deep engagement in the creative process, we cultivate their leadership and creative thinking. We are also committed to building an open, inclusive inter-school performance platform rooted in the secondary school community.

提供舞台讓年輕表演者展現其獨特的風格與創意，從而發掘並培育多元化的藝術人才。藉由學生深度參與創作過程，培養領導才能及創意思維。本團隊亦致力建立一個以中學社群為基礎、開放且包容的跨校表演平台。

Vision 願景

To build a lasting, influential student-led youth arts platform across Hong Kong, cultivating young artistic talents from an early age and enriching the local youth cultural atmosphere. We aspire to establish Hong Kong's premier student-led stage, empowering the next generation to redefine the cultural landscape and drive the city's creative economy through sustainable youth art development.

於香港建立一個可持續、具影響力且由學生主導的青年藝術平台，從小培育優秀青年藝術人才，豐富在地青年文化氛圍。本團隊期望打造香港首屈一指的學生主導舞台，賦能下一代代表演者，透過可持續的青年藝術發展，重新定義文化景觀並推動城市創意經濟。
